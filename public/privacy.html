Privacy Policy

How LocalShare Handles Your Data

Your files

LocalShare is a peer-to-peer file sharing tool. Files you share travel directly between devices on your local network over a secure WebSocket/WebRTC connection — they are never uploaded to or stored on our servers.

Advertising

LocalShare uses Google AdSense to display ads and help support the site. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage your ad personalization settings at adssettings.google.com.

If you are visiting from the EEA or UK, you may see a consent message asking for permission to use cookies for personalized advertising.

Suggestions

If you submit feedback through our suggestions form, your message is emailed to us and is not stored or shared beyond that.
